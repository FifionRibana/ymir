- **E4 held**: never collision only.

  (`subduction/source_term.rs:266–268`, s < 0.05), not rift strips. They weigh 5–15 % of the active coast cells.
- **The cordilleras' width**: young 15–30 km world (110–225 km terrestrial); narrower at the old age.
- **The adoption rule**: met at both ages ((v) ≥ 5, no rampart, the circumnavigation). But the cordilleras narrow and thin at
  the old age, so the plates go to the author before the adoption is made effective (the complement).
